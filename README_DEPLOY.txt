ETSU 2026 SUMMIT — APPALACHIAN HIGHLANDS SUPPLY CHAIN CHALLENGE (EXPANDED EDITION)

PURPOSE
A promotional business/supply-chain simulation for the 2026 Appalachian Highlands
Supply Chain and Digital Technologies Summit.

WHAT CHANGED IN THIS EDITION
- More detail about the fictional company and its operating model.
- More scenario background before every decision.
- Choices use supply-chain terminology and strategic decision frameworks.
- Each outcome explains why the scoring logic makes sense.
- Final page includes all people identifiable from the current public Eventbrite
  lineup/agenda plus the event organizer, with concise bios and source links.
- Added a visible "decision logic" section explaining the external professional
  guidance used to validate the scenarios.

FICTIONAL COMPANY
RidgeLine Systems is a fictional Appalachian Highlands B2B advanced manufacturer of
connected monitoring units, control enclosures, and replacement components for
healthcare, industrial OEM, and commercial customers. All RidgeLine figures, scores,
costs, and outcomes are teaching/promotional assumptions.

FIVE DECISION DOMAINS
1. Strategic sourcing and resilience:
   Time-to-survive vs. time-to-recover, dual sourcing, inventory segmentation,
   targeted premium freight, total cost of disruption.

2. Constrained-supply allocation / S&OP:
   Available-to-promise, customer criticality, contractual priorities, expedite
   capacity, re-promising, and cross-functional exception governance.

3. AI portfolio selection:
   Business-value x feasibility, data readiness, measurable KPI baseline,
   exception-management workflow, human-in-the-loop deployment.

4. Lean Six Sigma + Theory of Constraints:
   Constraint protection, DMAIC, Pareto/root-cause analysis, first-pass yield,
   rework, effective capacity, standard work and control plans.

5. Critical thinking + AI governance:
   Human-in-the-loop oversight, model context, missing variables, supplier
   qualification, scenario/sensitivity analysis, and explicit decision rights.

DECISION-LOGIC VALIDATION
The game logic was checked against:
- McKinsey supply-chain resilience work on dual sourcing, regionalization,
  visibility, inventory and structural resilience:
  https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/supply-chain-risk-survey-2024

- Gartner supply-chain AI guidance emphasizing business value + feasibility,
  data readiness and avoiding "AI for the sake of AI":
  https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-05-21-gartner-supply-chain-symposium-xpo-barcelona-day-3-highlights

- ASQ DMAIC guidance requiring Define, Measure and Analyze/root cause before
  Improve and Control:
  https://asq.org/quality-resources/dmaic

- NIST AI Risk Management Framework guidance on context, model limitations,
  human roles and oversight:
  https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/appendices/app-c-ai-risk-management-and-human-ai-interaction/
